Hazards found

HZ-001 · A still-valid action is destroyed by a permissionless trigger in the same block

class 2 ordering confirmed on this target

Two exits are legal in the same block and pay opposite amounts. The transaction that lands first decides, and the losing exit cannot be repaired because the other is already dead from its own clock.

What is at stake: one deal amount, which is the worker's entire payment on the affected deal.

What to do: Bound the still-valid exit by the same clock as the permissionless trigger, or document which exit is authoritative after the window.

Reproduced here: confirmations/HZ-001-c2292af8.json — a rebuild of the verified source and the probes in it passed. Confirming behaviour, not intent.

HZ-009 · Two permissionless exits are both legal in one block and pay opposite amounts

class 2 ordering confirmed on this target

Inside one block a stranger trigger and the buyer exit are both valid and settle the deal differently. Block ordering alone decides who is paid, and the loser cannot repair afterwards because the other path is already closed by its own clock.

What is at stake: one deal: full price on one ordering, zero on the other.

What to do: Define the authoritative exit for the overlap, or bound both paths by the same clock.

Reproduced here: confirmations/HZ-009-c2292af8.json — a rebuild of the verified source and the probes in it passed. Confirming behaviour, not intent.

HZ-005 · Value arriving outside the accounting has no sweep

class 7 stranded-value confirmed on this target 3 absence checks

A contract that pays out only from internal credits accepts forced native value or direct token transfers, counts them in no total, and offers no function to recover them. Accounting stays correct, so nothing breaks and nothing notices.

What is at stake: the stranded amount itself, unbounded by any ceiling.

What to do: Add a sweep for stray value, or document that it is donated.

Reproduced here: confirmations/HZ-005-c2292af8.json — a rebuild of the verified source and the probes in it passed. Confirming behaviour, not intent.
